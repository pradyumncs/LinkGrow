# SEO Title Optimization Standards

Whenever generating or editing content titles, follow these non-negotiable rules:

1. **Exact-Match Front-Loading**: Start with what users type into Google search.
2. **Character Constraint**: Keep total length between 45 and 60 characters to prevent Google SERP truncation.
3. **Parenthetical Hook**: Include a parenthetical benefit, warning, or year at the end `(...)` to maximize CTR.
4. **Zero Fluff**: Omit passive voice, generic intros, and branding prefixes in the title tag.
