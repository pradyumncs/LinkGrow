# LinkGrow Project Rules & Guidelines

## 🎯 Strict SEO Title Architecture Rule

Whenever generating, updating, or reviewing titles for blog articles, guides, landing pages, or metadata, ALWAYS enforce the following SEO title standards:

### 1. Front-Load the Exact-Match Search Query
* The first 3 to 6 words **MUST** be the exact search phrase that real users type into Google or search engines.
* **Never** lead with branding, clickbait fluff, or company intros.
* **Bad**: *A Comprehensive Security Teardown of Fake Calculator Apps on iOS*
* **Good**: *Calculator Photo Vault Apps: Are They Safe? (2026 Review)*

### 2. The 55–60 Character SERP Limit
* Google truncates desktop titles at ~600 pixels (typically 55–60 characters).
* Keep titles under **60 characters** whenever possible so the full title and call-to-action remain visible in search engine result pages (SERPs) without being cut off by `...`.

### 3. The 3 Winning High-CTR Title Archetypes
Every title must follow one of these three formulas:

1. **The Anxiety / Direct Question Formula**:
   `[Exact Search Question]? ([Key Outcome / Warning])`
   *Example*: `Does CarPlay Show Hidden Photos? (How to Prevent Leaks)`
   *Example*: `Are Hidden Photos Saved to iCloud? (The Truth)`

2. **The High-Intent How-To / Solution Formula**:
   `How to [Exact Intent Keyword] ([Specific Method / Tool])`
   *Example*: `How to Delete Photos from iPhone but Keep in iCloud`
   *Example*: `How to Tell If Someone Opened Your Hidden Album`

3. **The Commercial / Best-In-Class Formula**:
   `Best [Category] for [Platform] ([Parenthetical Power Hook])`
   *Example*: `Best One-Time Purchase Photo Vault for iPhone (No Subscriptions)`

### 4. High-CTR Modifiers & Power Hooks
* Use parenthetical tags `(...)` at the end of the title: `(iOS 18)`, `(2026)`, `(Step-by-Step)`, `(No Subscription)`, `(How to Fix)`.
* Tests show parenthetical hooks boost organic click-through rates (CTR) by up to 33%.

### 5. URL Slug & Heading Consistency
* The URL slug must be an exact, clean reflection of the primary keyword: e.g., `/guides/does-carplay-show-your-photos`.
* The `<h1>` on the page must match the `<title>` tag closely to satisfy Google's search intent relevance.
